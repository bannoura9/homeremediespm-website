## The quality bar — what "complete" means

A half-filled listing is close to worthless. Before you tell me a platform is done,
confirm every one of these that the platform supports:

- [ ] Business name matches the block **exactly**
- [ ] Full street address, not just the city
- [ ] Phone matches exactly, including format
- [ ] Website URL uses `https://www.` — not the bare domain, not `http://`
- [ ] Primary category set, secondary if available
- [ ] Full description filled in, not left blank or truncated mid-sentence
- [ ] All service areas or as many as the platform allows
- [ ] All services listed
- [ ] Business hours set for every day
- [ ] Every photo slot the platform offers is filled
- [ ] Logo uploaded
- [ ] Any "attributes" or checkbox features reviewed and set honestly
- [ ] Social links added if the platform has fields for them
- [ ] Listing is actually **published/live**, not saved as a draft

Then tell me which of these you could not complete and why.

## Report back after each platform

Keep a running summary and give it to me at the end. For each platform:

- Status — live / pending verification / blocked
- The public URL of the listing once it exists
- Anything you could not fill in, and what you need from me
- Any field where the platform forced a value different from my data block

Finish with a list of everything still outstanding and what each item is waiting on.

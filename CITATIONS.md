# Citation & Link Build — Home Remedies Property Management

**Why this file exists.** Google Search Console reports **1 external link** to the entire
domain, and it comes from `xploredomains.com` — a scraper, not a real citation. That single
number explains the average position of 48.6. The site has 20 city pages, 15 articles and a
96% technical health score; none of it can outrank competitors who have hundreds of links.

Content is done. This is the work that is left.

---

## The canonical NAP block

Every listing must use these **exactly**. Character-for-character. Inconsistent name,
address or phone across directories is worse than no listing at all — Google treats the
variants as different businesses and splits the trust between them.

```
Business name:  Home Remedies Property Management
Street:         3923 West 99th Pl.
City/State/ZIP: Westminster, CO 80031
Phone:          (303) 515-1352
Website:        https://www.homeremediespm.com
Email:          homeremediespm@gmail.com
Primary category: Property Management Company
Secondary:      Real Estate Agency
```

**Do not** use "Home Remedies PM", "Home Remedies Property Mgmt", the old Arvada address,
or the (720) 722-0357 number anywhere. Those are the exact variants that cause the split.

## Tier 3 — aggregators

These feed data to dozens of smaller directories automatically, so each one is worth
several listings. Lower individual value, good leverage.

Foursquare · Manta · Hotfrog · Cylex · Brownbook · EZlocal · Chamberofcommerce.com ·
MerchantCircle · Superpages · Yellowpages.com

## Tier 4 — local links, hardest and most valuable

A single link from a real Westminster institution outweighs the whole of Tier 3. These
are what actually separate a local business from a directory listing.

- **Westminster Chamber of Commerce** — paid membership, real `.org` local link
- **North Metro Denver Chamber** — same
- **Sponsor something local** — a youth sports team, a school event, a neighborhood
  association newsletter. Sponsors nearly always get a link from the organizer's site,
  and it is the most defensible local link there is.
- **Colorado Association of REALTORS** — if Nadir holds a license, the member directory
  listing is already available and often unclaimed.

competitors sit between 55 and 710. The rating is already better than every one of them
— the volume is the only gap.

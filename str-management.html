Is short-term rental legal for my property?

This is the first question to answer, and it depends entirely on where your property is. Denver and Boulder are the two most restrictive jurisdictions on our patch — both limit short-term rental licenses to a property that is the operator's primary residence, which rules out pure investment properties inside those specific city limits. Other Front Range cities, unincorporated county areas, and HOA-free properties often have different rules.

We check this for your exact address as part of the free consultation, before you spend anything on furnishing or setup. If your property isn't eligible for short-term rental where it sits, we'll tell you plainly and talk through long-term management instead — a licensing rule doesn't change what we'll recommend.

Short-term vs. long-term: which is right for your property?

They're genuinely different businesses, not just different lease lengths. Short-term rental can produce meaningfully higher revenue per property, but it comes with more moving parts — turnover coordination, guest communication at all hours, seasonal demand swings, and stricter local licensing. Long-term management is steadier and lower-touch, with one tenant and one lease to manage for months or years at a time.

If you're weighing the two, our free rental analysis covers what your property could earn as a long-term lease, and we'll walk through the short-term revenue picture in the same consultation — so you're comparing real numbers, not guesses.

Frequently asked questions

How much does short-term rental management cost?

One flat 20% of booking revenue. That's at the lower end of the 20-30% full-service range most Denver-metro Airbnb and VRBO management companies charge, and there's no separate setup fee, onboarding fee, or cleaning markup layered on top.

Which platforms do you list on?

Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com, kept in sync from one calendar so you never risk a double-booking. We handle the listing copy, photography coordination, and pricing on all three.

Is short-term rental legal for my property?

It depends on your city. Denver and Boulder, among others, restrict short-term rental licenses to an operator's primary residence, which rules out pure investment properties in those specific jurisdictions. Other Front Range cities and unincorporated areas have different rules. We check eligibility for your exact address as part of the free consultation before you commit to anything.

What's actually included in the 20%?

Multi-platform listing and calendar sync, dynamic pricing, guest communication and screening, turnover coordination between guests, and maintenance oversight. Cleaning is billed at cost, passed through with no markup, since it varies by property size and turnover frequency.

How is this different from your long-term property management?

Long-term management places a tenant on a lease and manages that relationship for months or years. Short-term rental management runs your property as a nightly or weekly rental across Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com, with a new guest cycling through every few days. The pricing, work, and legal rules for the two are different, which is why we run them as separate services.

How do I get paid?

Booking revenue comes in through each platform, and we pass your share through to you on a regular schedule along with a statement showing occupancy, nightly rate, and what was collected.

## PART 2 — Ask reviewers to cross-post

This is the higher-leverage move: these are already happy, already-written
testimonials. Turning even a handful of them into Yelp / BBB / Nextdoor / Google
reviews-on-other-platforms directly attacks the site's real bottleneck — GSC
shows **1 external backlink to the whole domain**, while competitors have
hundreds of quality referring domains (see `CITATIONS.md`). A review on a
high-authority directory is both a trust signal and a citation.

**Template (personalize the bracketed parts, send individually — a batch/BCC
blast reads as spam and gets ignored):**

> Subject: Quick favor — 30 seconds?
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you again for the kind words you left us on Google — it really made our
> day. If you don't mind, would you be open to sharing the same thought on
> [Yelp / Nextdoor / BBB]? It takes about 30 seconds and helps a lot more than
> you'd think for a small, family-run business like ours.
>
> [Direct link once each listing is live — see CITATIONS.md Tier 1]
>
> No worries at all if not — just wanted to ask. Thanks again for trusting us
> with [your home / your rental]!
>
> — Nadir

**Best candidates to start with** (varied service type — landlord, tenant,
buyer — so the new platforms don't look one-dimensional):

1. Justin Johnson (Landlord) — strongest, most detailed review
2. Nermin S. (Home buyer) — covers the buy-side of the business
3. Jill V. (Tenant) — short, easy first ask
4. Feras M. (Client) — mentions negotiation/transparency, good for BBB tone

**Sequencing:** send this *after* the Yelp/BBB/Nextdoor listings exist (Tier 1
in `CITATIONS.md`) — asking someone to review a listing that doesn't exist yet
doesn't work. Claim/create the listing first, then ask.

Common questions

Resident questions

When can I pay rent online?

Online rent payment opens with the Buildium Resident Center on January 1, 2027. Until then, pay the way your lease describes. If you are not sure where a payment should go, call or text (303) 515-1352.

How do I submit a maintenance request?

Send a non-emergency request through the contact page and describe the issue. For no heat, an active leak, a gas smell, or anything that makes the home unsafe, call (303) 515-1352. That number is the 24/7 emergency maintenance line.

When does the resident portal open?

The online resident portal launches January 1, 2027. The Buildium Resident Center is where residents will pay rent, submit maintenance requests, and view lease documents. The sign-in link will be posted on this page when it is live.

How do I apply for an available home?

Open the available homes on our properties page and apply from the listing. Applicants may provide a Colorado portable tenant screening report that meets the state's requirements.

Who do I call after hours?

Call (303) 515-1352. The office is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. The same number is answered 24/7 for emergency maintenance.

Need a person, not a portal?

Call or text (303) 515-1352. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, and the emergency maintenance line is 24/7.

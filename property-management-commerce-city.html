Commerce City Property Management

Home Remedies is your local Commerce City property management company — full-service management for property owners, from tenant screening and leasing to rent collection, maintenance, property inspections and monthly financial reporting.

Commerce City is really two rental markets sharing one name. South of 72nd, Original Commerce City is older, affordable and remarkably steady — the kind of housing that never sits empty for long. North of 104th, master-planned communities like Reunion and Buffalo Run are full of newer single-family homes and townhomes with a very different renter and a very different rent. Add a short E-470 run to DIA and the jobs clustered around it, and you get some of the most consistent renter demand in the north metro.

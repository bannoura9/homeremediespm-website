Inherited a house in Colorado? What to settle before you rent or sell

The first question is not rent versus sell. It is whether you have authority to do either, and whether the insurance still covers the house while you decide.

By Nadir Maaiah, Home Remedies Property Management · Westminster, CO·November 21, 2026·8 min read

The short answer

  • An heir is not automatically the person who can sign a lease, list the house, or collect rent. Authority is a probate, trust, or title question for an attorney.
  • Call the insurer before you decide anything else. A vacant inherited house is often a different risk than the policy that was on it.
  • An existing lease usually continues. So do the deposit and habitability duties.
  • Renting still needs the city's license or registration. Selling and renting are both legitimate. The numbers have to be real, and the tax basis is a CPA's work.

Settling an estate? This page is a practical sequence for heirs who might become landlords in Westminster, Arvada, Thornton, Northglenn, Broomfield, or north Denver. It is not probate advice and not tax advice. For the house as a rental, request a free rental analysis. For a conversation about the property, contact us.

Inheriting a house feels like a decision between a "for rent" sign and a "for sale" sign. The first decision is whether you have the legal power to do either. Families lose months, and sometimes the insurance, by showing the property and collecting rent before anyone has authority to act. None of the stories in this guide are client stories. There aren't any. The sequence is the same one a title company, an insurer, and a tenant will force you through anyway.

Who is allowed to act

A death does not, by itself, put the heir's name on the deed or give that heir the right to bind the estate. Depending on how the house was owned, the person who can sign may be a personal representative appointed by the court, a successor trustee, a surviving joint owner, or someone else. Wills, beneficiary deeds, and trusts each take a different path, and intestacy is its own path. A listing agreement, a lease, or a repair contract signed by the wrong person is a problem for the signer and for the person on the other side.

Take the death certificate, the will or trust, and the last deed to a Colorado estate attorney and to the title company before you advertise. This guide will not tell you which probate shortcut applies. That is the lawyer's job, and getting it wrong is more expensive than the consultation. If a sibling disagrees, stop. Do not list the house "to get it resolved."

Insurance and a vacant house

Call the agent who wrote the current policy the week you learn of the death. Ask, in writing, what the policy does when the occupant has died and when the house is vacant. Homeowner policies are written for someone living there. Vacancy is often treated as a different risk, and the number of days the carrier uses is in that policy, not in a blog. Do not guess it. Ask whether a landlord policy, a vacant-dwelling policy, or an endorsement is what they will actually issue, and what they will not cover in the meantime. Frozen pipes, vandalism, and liability for a visitor who slips on the walk are the reasons this call comes before the open house.

If the house is already rented, tell the carrier that too. A tenant in place is not the same fact as a vacant house, and the policy may need to be a landlord form rather than the decedent's homeowner form. Our landlord insurance guide is background. The declarations page is the document that matters.

If someone already lives there

A lease generally survives a change of ownership. The tenant's right to stay, the rent amount, and the deposit terms are in the contract the decedent signed, subject to Colorado law. You do not get a fresh start because you inherited the house. You also do not get to change the locks, shut off utilities, or pocket the deposit because the owner died.

Once someone with authority is in place, that person steps into the landlord's duties. That includes returning or accounting for the security deposit under Colorado's rules, which our deposit guide summarizes, and keeping the home habitable, which our habitability guide summarizes. Give the tenant a written notice of who now collects rent and where, after counsel confirms you are that person. Selling with a tenant in place has its own constraints, covered in selling a rental with tenants. Self-help eviction is not one of the options. If the occupant has no lease and will not leave, that is an attorney problem, not a weekend project. Our no-lease eviction guide explains why the label "squatter" is usually the wrong one.

Condition, make-ready, and the city's permission to rent

Walk the house once you have authority and a right to enter. Photograph the roof, the furnace, the water heater, active leaks, and anything that would matter to a buyer or a tenant. A rental that is not habitable does not get to wait on a renovation plan while someone is living there. If it is vacant, the make-ready list is how you compare "rent" with "sell." Paint and carpet are one kind of cost. A furnace, a roof, and a sewer line are another. Get contractor numbers before you fall in love with either path.

Renting also needs the local license. Denver licenses residential rentals of 30 days or more. Westminster registers many single-family homes and licenses properties with four or more units, with wording on the city page that is broader than that split. Read our Westminster rental license guide and the city-by-city license guide before you advertise. An inherited house in Thornton, Northglenn, or Broomfield follows that city's program, not Westminster's. The overview pages for Thornton, Northglenn, and Broomfield are a starting point, not the ordinance.

Rent or sell, without a fake spreadsheet

Four questions decide it. None of them is a universal answer.

  • Cash flow. Rent minus the mortgage, if any, taxes, insurance, repairs, vacancy, and the cost of your time. A free rental analysis gives you a rent figure grounded in local leases. It does not give you net income. Across the industry, monthly management is often discussed in a range of about 8 to 12 percent of collected rent. That is market language, not a quote from this company and not a forecast. Our pricing is on the pricing page, and a published schedule does not change a contract you later sign unless that contract says so.
  • Tax basis. Inherited property has federal income-tax rules, including how basis is set at death, that a CPA has to apply to this house and this estate. This page will not estimate a gain, a depreciation schedule, or whether a 1031 exchange is even available. Do not list the house to "avoid taxes" on a relative's advice.
  • The market you are actually in. A north-metro ranch and a Denver condo do not share a buyer pool or a renter pool. Our sell-or-rent guide walks the Denver version of the choice. Use it as a framework, then price this address.
  • Whether you can be the landlord. Distance, a full-time job, and siblings who disagree are operational facts. Our out-of-state landlord guide is written for that constraint. If you live more than 50 miles from a Westminster rental, the city's license and registration forms require a local agent. That requirement is described in the Westminster license guide.

Holding the house because selling feels disloyal, or selling it because landlording feels like too much, are both human reasons. They are not a substitute for the insurance call, the authority question, and a rent number you did not invent. If you want that number, ask for the analysis. If you want to talk through the property with someone local, write or call.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sign a lease on a house I just inherited?

Only if you have authority to act for the estate, trust, or title. A Colorado estate attorney should confirm that before you advertise. Being named in a will is not the same thing as being appointed.

What happens to the tenant who was already there?

The lease generally continues. Deposit and habitability duties continue with it. Do not change the locks or redirect rent until counsel confirms who the landlord is.

Does an inherited house need a rental license?

If you rent it, yes, in cities that license or register rentals. Denver and Westminster both do, on different terms. Check the city before the ad goes up.

Will I owe capital gains if I sell?

Maybe, maybe not. Basis at death is a federal tax question for a CPA who has the estate documents. This guide does not calculate it.
